== Description ==

Movers Agency is a versatile and powerful theme designed specifically for businesses in the relocation and moving industry, including packing services, logistics companies, freight movers, local movers, long-distance moving services, office relocation services, residential movers, vehicle transport services, storage facilities, courier and delivery services, and professional relocation agencies. With its intuitive design and robust features, this theme offers everything you need to create a professional and engaging online presence for your moving, packing, or logistics company. Built with the latest web technologies, the Movers Agency theme ensures seamless performance and compatibility across all devices and screen sizes. The theme comes with a range of customizable elements, including pre-designed layouts, color schemes, and typography options, allowing you to tailor the look and feel of your website to match your brand identity. Whether you are a local mover or a global relocation service provider, the Movers Agency theme offers flexibility and versatility to meet your specific needs. With built-in features such as service pages, pricing tables, booking forms, and contact forms, you can showcase your range of services, highlight your pricing packages, and make it easy for potential customers to get in touch. Additionally, the theme includes integration with popular plugins like WooCommerce and Contact Form 7, allowing you to set up an online store for moving supplies or enable seamless customer inquiries. The Movers Agency theme also prioritizes user experience, mobile-friendly layouts, and SEO-friendliness, ensuring that your website not only looks great but also ranks well in search engine results, helping you attract more organic traffic and grow your relocation business online.

== Installation ==
        
1. In your admin panel, go to Appearance > Themes and click the Add New button.
2. Click Upload Theme and Choose File, then select the packers-agency theme's .zip file. Click Install Now.
3. Click Activate to use your new theme right away
